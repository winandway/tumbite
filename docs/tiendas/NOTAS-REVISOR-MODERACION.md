# Notas para el revisor de Apple: moderación de contenido (regla 1.2)

Preparado el 16 nov 2026 (C-MODERACION). Se pegan en App Store Connect → la versión → «App Review Information» → «Notes», en la PRÓXIMA versión de cada app que tenga chat o reseñas (Divoox y Divoox Driver; Divoox Restaurants si se declara contenido de usuarios). La versión del cliente que ya está en revisión no se toca. Nada va a revisión sin el APTO del auditor de tiendas y la decisión de Richard.

## Texto para Divoox (cliente), en inglés (el revisor lee inglés)

```
User-generated content and safety (Guideline 1.2):
- Chat exists only between the customer and the courier/driver of an active order or ride, and closes when it ends. No phone numbers are shared.
- Report: every message from the other person and every public restaurant review (and the restaurant's reply) has a "Report" button. Users pick a reason (offensive, harassment or threat, spam, other) and can add a note. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours; the reported person never learns who reported them.
- Block: from the three-dots menu in the chat, users can block the other person. They stop seeing that person's messages and notifications, and we won't match them again on orders or rides. Blocked people are listed under Account > Blocked people, with Unblock.
- Filter: an English/Spanish word filter masks offensive words in chat and holds reviews containing them until our team reviews them.
- Moderation: our support team has a "Flagged content" inbox to hide content, warn the author or suspend the account.
- Contact: soporte+divoox@windoce.com and Help inside the app.
How to test: sign in with the demo customer account, open an active order, and use "Report" under a driver message or the three dots > Block. Reviews with "Report" are on any restaurant page with reviews.
```

## Texto para Divoox Driver, en inglés

```
User-generated content and safety (Guideline 1.2):
- Chat exists only between the driver and the customer of an active delivery or ride, and closes when it ends. No phone numbers are shared.
- Report: every customer message has a "Report" button with a reason (offensive, harassment or threat, spam, other) and an optional note. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours; the reported person never learns who reported them.
- Block: from the three-dots menu in the chat, drivers can block a customer. They stop seeing that customer's messages and won't be offered that customer's orders or rides again. Blocked people are listed under Account > Blocked people, with Unblock.
- Filter: an English/Spanish word filter masks offensive words in chat.
- Contact: soporte+divoox@windoce.com and Help inside the app.
```

## Texto para Divoox Restaurants, en inglés (si se declara contenido de usuarios)

```
Restaurants can report any customer review from Reviews > Report (reason + optional note). Our team reviews it within 24 hours and hides it if it breaks our guidelines. Restaurant replies go through an English/Spanish word filter; replies with offensive words are not published. Contact: soporte+divoox@windoce.com.
```
