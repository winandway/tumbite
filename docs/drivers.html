    For drivers

    Invite drivers and build your team: earn balance for each oneWith your code or link you invite other drivers. When your invitee completes their first trips, you earn balance. If you lead a group, your team stays linked to you and you see how many are signed up, approved and active.Your balance in Divoox Driver: how it works, how to top up and what each trip takes offIn cities where the rider pays you everything in person, the Divoox commission comes out of a balance you top up in advance. Without balance you don’t get trips. Here’s how it works and how to top it up.Cash out your earnings with a Divoox AgentIn cities with a Divoox Agent, you can collect your earnings in cash. Get a code in your app, go to the agent, show it, and they hand you the money.Top up your balance in cash with a Divoox AgentIf your city has a Divoox Agent, you can top up your commission balance by paying cash at their location. The balance arrives right away.Trips with a Divoox gift: collect the difference and Divoox pays the restWhen the rider has a Divoox gift, you collect in person only the difference. Divoox pays you what the gift covers: it goes to your earnings when the trip ends.Destination mode: orders on your way homeSet your destination —home, for example— and Divoox Driver only offers you orders whose drop-off takes you closer. You can use it twice a day, and it turns off on its own when you arrive.My route: delivering company packages with Divoox DriverCompanies assign or offer you package routes. You accept, load by scanning every package, follow the stops in order, deliver with a photo, PIN or signature, and bring back to the hub whatever couldn’t be delivered.Your location with the screen offWhile you’re online, Divoox keeps sending your position even if you lock the phone or open the map: the customer and the restaurant see where you are without you coming back to the app.Deliveries with an age checkIf the order has items for people over 21, you hand it over in person, check the ID and note the ID type, its last four digits and the name of the person receiving.Orders collected in person and your balance for feesIn cities where it’s active, some orders are paid on delivery: the customer pays you in cash or by transfer, you pay for the food at the counter, and Divoox’s fee comes out of a balance you top up in advance.When an offer reaches you and how you pick up with the codeOffers reach you so you arrive when the food is ready, not before. At the restaurant you scan the code on their screen and take the photo: the order goes out on its own.Complete the delivery: photo and slideAt the customer’s door, take the delivery photo and slide “Slide to complete delivery.” That closes the order, just like the biggest delivery apps.If you can’t continue with an order or the customer isn’t thereBefore pickup you can release the order with a reason, no penalty. At the door, if the customer isn’t there, there’s a 5-minute protocol.Start deliveringCreate your account, go online when you want, and accept only what works for you.Verify your identityYour ID and a photo of your face, right from the app. It’s what gives customers peace of mind when they open the door.Activated for Food: vehicle and documentsTo get Food orders, your account has to be activated for Food and, if you deliver by car or motorcycle, have an approved vehicle and a current license and insurance. If something is missing, the app tells you when you go online.Offers: see what you earn before you acceptYou see the pay, the distance, and the pickup spot. Declining never counts against you.My ratings: what customers see about youUnder Profile → “My ratings” you see your customer rating, your acceptance, completion, and on-time rates, and how many deliveries you have. With the big apps’ reference next to each.Earnings breakdown: base pay, bonuses, and tipsUnder Earnings you see, by day, week, or month, how much was base pay, how much bonuses, and how much tips, and every order with its breakdown. A tip that arrives after delivery is added there too.The bell and pause: your notifications and when not to receive offersThe bell shows your real notifications from the last seven days: order ready, cancellations, address changes, tips, check-in photo. And with “Pause offers” you stay online without receiving offers.“Slide when you reach the restaurant”, “order isn’t ready”, and help with the orderThe active order has three new buttons: to say you arrived at the restaurant, to record that the order isn’t ready, and to get help with clear reasons or call emergency services.Levels: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and DiamondHigher levels see bigger orders first. You level up with deliveries and good ratings.Your wallet: what you earn and how you cash outLike a banking app: your balance, earnings, tips, and every movement with its order. Cash out to your bank whenever you want.Your face photo and your documentsIn Divoox Driver, your face photo comes first: with it you can go online and work while you upload the rest of your documents. It takes a moment, with the app’s camera.What you need to get tripsIf trips aren’t coming in yet, Divoox Driver tells you at the top of the home screen what you still need, one thing at a time, with a button that takes you straight there.Driving with Divoox MotoGet ride offers with your earnings upfront, pick up the rider with their code and end the ride in the app.Register your motorcycle and documents for Divoox MotoChoose your city, register your motorcycle, upload your documents with your camera, and track your approval in the app.How you’re paid for Moto ridesOn a cash ride the rider pays you the full fare at the end, and the app shows the amount at every step. A prepaid fee balance is only used in some countries, not in the United States.Driver rest and safetyAfter 12 hours of driving, you rest for 6 before getting more rides. Every ride has an emergency button.Sign up for Divoox Car: your car and your documentsRegister your car with its seats, upload the car’s documents and your own, and track your approval in the app.Receive and complete Divoox Car ridesAccept the offer, pick up the rider, start with their code, and collect at the end based on the payment method.Your car’s category: Car, Comfort, and PremiumYour car gets rides in its category, which pay more, and you decide whether to also get rides from the categories below.Rides with stops and with dynamic pricingThe offer tells you if the ride has stops or dynamic pricing. At each stop you mark that you arrived and continue when your rider is back.How much I collect and how much is mineWhen the rider pays the Divoox service fee, you collect it in hand along with the ride. Your app tells you how much you collect, how much is yours and how much goes to Divoox, before you accept.Your regular riders and scheduleRiders who choose you as their trusted driver reach you first when they request, and their regular rides show up in your weekly schedule.Register your taxi or mototaxi lineIf you run a line of taxis or mototaxis, register it on Divoox: you are the leader, your drivers ask to join through you and, if one of them asks for help, you hear about it first.Your line in Divoox DriverJoin your group’s line with its code, or choose it from the list for your city. Your leader accepts you and, if you ask for help, gets your location.Go online for several services at onceIn Divoox Driver you go online for every service you’re approved for at the same time: food, deliveries and rides. You get offers from all of them, and when you accept one, the others pause on their own until you finish it.Add a service without re-entering your infoYour profile works for all your services. To add a new one, Divoox only asks for what that service still needs: anything we’ve already approved isn’t asked again.At the customer’s door: handing over the orderIn the United States orders are already paid in the app, so there is nothing to collect at the door: you hand over the order with the customer’s PIN, and the app tells you what to do if they aren’t there.Fee balance and top-ups: not needed in the United StatesIn the United States customers pay in the app, so you don’t keep a fee balance or buy top-ups: what you earn on each delivery shows under Earnings.Delivering packages with Divoox DeliveryPick up the package, take a photo of it and deliver it with the recipient’s PIN.Packages by size and delivery photoYou get deliveries that fit your vehicle, and you can leave a photo at drop-off.Check the door photo before you arriveWhen the customer saved photos of their entrance, you see them on the order card while you’re on the way: find the right door without calling.Your Divoox Flex block: from offer to payA Flex block is a 1 h 30 min to 5 hour shift to deliver a company’s packages along a route. While you do it, everything else is off. When you close it, you see your summary and your pay.Your deadline to submit your documentsWhere Divoox gives a deadline, you start working with your face photo and upload the rest of your documents afterward, with time to spare and reminders.Your profile for ridersSee how riders see you during their trip and complete it with the languages you speak and something about you.
