    Ordering

    Is Divoox in my area? Notify me when it arrivesAnyone, in any country, can create an account. If Divoox isn’t in your area yet, the app tells you and emails you the day it arrives.Shop at the grocery store, pharmacy or liquor storeIn Grocery, Pharmacy, Liquor and More you see the stores near you, search a product in all of them at once and choose what happens if something is out.Items sold by weight: you pay the actual weightFruit, vegetables and meat are ordered at an estimated weight and charged at what they actually weigh, never more than 15% over.Your order alerts and how to call the driver without sharing your numberWe let you know when the driver is 5 minutes away, when to come out (if you chose hand delivery) and when they’re at your door, also by SMS. And you can call them with a masked number.Your orders: history, receipt and reorderIn the Orders tab you see each order with its status and total, open the full receipt and order the same again with one tap.Schedule an order for laterPick the day and the half hour when you want your order, from one hour ahead up to six days out.Group order: everyone picks their ownBuild one order for the office, family or friends: share a link, each person adds their items and you pay for everything together.Share your order trackingSend a link so someone else can see how your order is going, without an account and without seeing your details.“You might like”: new restaurants for youOn the home screen we show open, nearby restaurants of the kind of food you already ordered that you haven’t tried yet.Favorites: save your restaurantsTap a restaurant’s heart to save it to Favorites and find it instantly next time.Your saved places: Home, Work and your ownSave Home, Work and the places you go often, with a name, an icon and the exact spot. It’s one list for every service: Moto, Car, Delivery and Food.The exact spot: “I’m here,” the fixed pin and your landmarkOn long avenues, an address alone isn’t enough. Move the map to your door, tap “I’m here” if you’re already there, and add a landmark: the driver comes straight to you.Divoox gifts: add your code and pay less in personDivoox can gift you part of your ride, delivery or order. Add the code in the app once: it applies on its own and you pay in person only the difference. Divoox pays the gift, not your driver.Gift cards: give Divoox and redeem a codeBuy a gift card for someone in the amount you choose. They get a code by email, and redeeming it adds a balance for their orders.Invite a friend: credit for both of youShare your personal code or link. Your friend gets a gift, and you get your reward once they complete their first paid orders or rides.Divoox Plus: free delivery and lower service fees for a monthly feeWith Divoox Plus you pay no delivery fee and a lower service fee on orders over the minimum. It renews every month and you can cancel anytime.Coupons and promotionsEnter a code in the cart and the discount applies before you pay. Current promotions show under “Today’s deals” on the home screen.Reporting a problem with your orderSomething missing, cold, late: tell us what happened in two taps. If an item was missing, you mark which one and, when enabled, only that value is refunded.Pay on delivery: cash or a transfer to the driverIn cities where it’s active, you can choose to pay when your order arrives: in cash to the driver, or with a transfer to the account they have published. Your order is confirmed instantly, no card needed.How you pay for your orderYou pay with your card inside the app, without leaving to another page. It’s authorized when you order and charged when it’s delivered.The home screen: what’s there and what each part doesAt the top, a banner with what’s new in your city; on top of it, where we deliver and the search bar. Below, Divoox services in four columns, deals that rotate on their own and restaurants near you.Searching Divoox: restaurants, dishes, and cuisinesTap the search bar on the home screen and type what you’re craving. As you type, Divoox searches your city and shows matching restaurants and dishes, open ones first.Dietary filters: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, and spicyRestaurants can label their dishes as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, or spicy. You see those labels on the menu and can filter search by them, with or without typing anything.The bell and the coupon: your notifications and your giftsThe bell on the home screen shows what’s happening with your account right now: active orders, gifts in progress, and upcoming openings in your city. The coupon opens your gifts directly.Divoox services: how to choose what you needOn the home screen you always see all eight Divoox services, in four columns. The ones that already work in your area open their own screen right away; the others let you ask for a heads-up. “More services” holds the rest: gifts, regular rides, trusted drivers and packages for you.Why don’t I see restaurants? What if the app won’t finish opening?The app always tells you what’s going on: no restaurants in your area yet, we’re not in your city yet, or the connection failed. And if it can’t verify your session, it lets you try again without losing your account.Pick up in store, change the address, and tip after deliveryThree things you can do with your order: pick it up yourself with no delivery fee, change the address before the driver leaves (seeing the new total), and leave an extra tip after delivery.Choose size and extras when orderingIf an item has options, when you add it you choose the size, extras or whatever the restaurant offers, and see what each adds.If the restaurant is out of an item in your orderNothing gets canceled outright: you decide whether to remove it (we refund that part) or cancel the whole order.Leave at door or hand it to me: you chooseWhen you order, you say whether to leave it at your door or hand it to you. The driver sees it and acts accordingly.Chat with your driverFrom the moment your order has a driver until it arrives, you can message them from the app, without sharing your number.When your order arrives: the estimate and the latest arrival timeYou see two times: in how many minutes it arrives and the latest arrival time. And we tell you when the driver is a minute away and at your door.How to place your first orderPick a nearby restaurant, build your cart, and pay with a card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.Track your order liveConfirmed, in the kitchen, on the way, and delivered, with your driver on the map.Cancel an order and get your money backUntil the restaurant accepts it, you cancel in one tap and get a full refund. And if the restaurant doesn’t answer, we cancel it for you.My account: your orders, cards, and detailsAll your orders and their status, the cards you saved, and your details, in one place in the app.Gifts: birthday, punch card, and win-backRestaurants reward you for coming back. Gifts show up in your cart on their own.Special instructions for an itemAsk for “no onions” or “well done” on each item, and the restaurant sees it in your order.See a restaurant’s ratingThe stars, how many ratings it has and what other customers wrote, before you order.Rate the food and the driverTwo separate ratings. If the food was late, it’s not the driver’s fault.Compliments and reasons when you rateWith 5 stars you pick what went well; with 3 or fewer, what went wrong. One tap, no typing.Request a motorcycle rideChoose where you’re going, see the price before you confirm, and follow your driver on the map until they arrive.Paying, canceling and tipping on MotoPay with cash or card, cancel for free at the start, and tip your driver when the ride ends.Your safety on ridesShare your ride live, choose trusted contacts and get help with one tap.Request a car (taxi) with Divoox CarRequest a Car or a six-seat Car XL, see the fixed price before you confirm, and follow your driver until they arrive.Pay, tip, and see your rides in CarPay by card or with cash, tip your driver when you arrive, and find the receipt and help in “My rides.”Choose your car: Car, Comfort, Premium, or XLSee each category’s price for your same ride and pick the one you want before you request.Add stops to your rideAdd stops before you request or once the ride is underway. You always see the new price before you confirm.Dynamic pricing: why the price is sometimes higherWhen lots of people are requesting rides and few drivers are available, the price goes up for a while. You see it before you request.While we find your driverAs soon as you request a ride or a delivery, you see a scene with the vehicle you chose, plus the route and the price. It usually takes less than a minute, and canceling is free while we search.Notifications during your rideAt every step of your ride or delivery you get a notification on your phone, even with the app closed: when your driver accepts, arrives, starts the ride, and finishes. They all stay in your bell.Notifications about your orderWe notify you on your phone, even with the app closed, at every step of your food order: when the restaurant accepts it, when it’s on the way, when your driver is almost there, and when they arrive. If something is canceled, we tell you what happens with your money.The Divoox service fee on your rideIn some cities, a separate line is added to your ride price: the Divoox service fee. You see it before you request, already included in the total, and it never changes afterward.Local fixed pricesOn some nearby trips, your city has a usual fixed price. If it is cheaper than the distance-based price, the app charges the local fixed price and tells you before you request.Request the same route againIn “My rides,” tap “Request the same route”: confirm the trip at today’s price and your driver heads your way, without typing the addresses again.Your trusted driverChoose a driver you’ve already ridden with and liked: when you request Moto, Car, or a delivery, your ride reaches them first.Regular rides: school, work, and moreSet up the ride you always take once (for example, school Monday to Friday at 7:00 and the ride back at noon) and Divoox requests it for you every day.How you pay for your food orderIn the United States you pay by card in the app when you order, in US dollars, and you see every charge before you confirm.If you weren’t there when the driver arrivedIf the driver arrives and can’t find you, we notify you and they wait a few minutes. If you don’t show up, the driver closes the order with a photo and you see what happened in your order.Send a package across townSend keys, documents or a gift from one place to another, as easily as requesting a ride.Receive a packageYou get a text with a link to follow the delivery live and your delivery PIN.Your package sizeEnvelope, small, medium or large: this tells us which driver can carry it.Edit a saved address: name, details and entrance photosGive each address any name you like, fix the apartment, the landmark or the spot on the map, and add entrance photos if you want.Add entrance photos so they find you on the first tryWith a few photos of your entrance and your door, your driver recognizes your house or building on the first try, even when the number is hard to see. It works the same when an order is coming to you and when you request a moto taxi or a ride from that address.Meet your driver before they arriveOn your moto or car ride you see who is picking you up: their photo, their vehicle and plate, their track record, and the languages they speak.
