## Versión en inglés (la que se envía)

**Subject:** Rebrand — planned replacement of our 3 apps with new package names (please confirm before we proceed)

Hello Google Play Developer Support team,

We are writing before taking any action, because we want to do this the right way and stay
fully within Google Play policies.

**Who we are.** Our developer account is "Windoce, LLC" (account ID 6061858998617775098), a
verified organization account (contact: go@windoce.com). Divoox is our food delivery and ride
marketplace. Under the Divoox brand we publish three apps, each for a different user:

| App                | Current package name            | Who uses it                       |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Divoox             | com.windoce.tumbite             | Customers who order food or rides |
| Divoox Driver      | com.windoce.tumbite.driver      | Couriers and drivers              |
| Divoox Restaurants | com.windoce.tumbite.restaurants | Restaurants and stores            |

**Why we need a change.** "Tumbite" was the internal code name of the project before we chose
our public brand, Divoox. The old name stayed inside the package names, so it now appears in
every public Play Store link. We understand that package names can never be changed, so the
only way to remove the old name is to publish the same three apps under new package names:

| Current                         | New                    |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| com.windoce.tumbite             | com.divoox.app         |
| com.windoce.tumbite.driver      | com.divoox.driver      |
| com.windoce.tumbite.restaurants | com.divoox.restaurants |

**What we will NOT do.** We will never keep two versions of the same app available to the
public at the same time. We understand the policy against multiple apps with highly similar
functionality, and this plan is designed so that it never happens.

**The steps we plan to follow, in this order:**

1. The current three apps stay published, unchanged, while the new ones are reviewed.
2. We create the three new apps in this same developer account, with the same content, the
   same store listing information, the same data safety answers and the same declarations
   (including the foreground service location declaration for Divoox Driver). We use managed
   publishing so that nothing goes live automatically after approval.
3. The day the three new apps are approved, we publish them and, on that same day, unpublish
   the three old apps. The old store listings will state that the app has moved to the new one.
4. Our existing testers and users receive an in-app notice with the link to the new app.
5. We do not delete the old apps and we never reuse their package names.

**Our questions:**

1. Is this plan acceptable under Google Play policies, so that the new apps are not treated as
   duplicate or repetitive content while both exist in our account during review?
2. Is there anything you would like us to add to the review notes or to the app content to make
   the replacement clear to the reviewer?
3. Is there an official process for a rebrand like this that we should follow instead?

We will not create or upload anything until we hear back from you. Thank you for your time and
guidance.

Kind regards,

The Divoox team — Windoce LLC
go@windoce.com · divoox.com

## Estado

- 15 nov 2026: carta enviada por Play Console → Ayuda → Crear ticket de asistencia (tema
  «Políticas de Google Play», «Otros problemas con mi cuenta de desarrollador»).
- El asistente de IA de Google respondió al instante que el plan «se ajusta a las políticas de
  Google Play y es la forma estándar de reemplazar apps al cambiar el identificador». Recomendó:
  activar la publicación administrada antes de enviar; en «Detalles de acceso → Otras
  instrucciones» de cada app nueva, decir que es un cambio de marca que reemplaza a
  com.windoce.tumbite (y las otras dos), que se retirarán al publicar; retirar las viejas en
  Configuración avanzada → Disponibilidad de la app.
- Igual se pidió la respuesta escrita de un especialista humano (botón «No, crear ticket»).
  Google dice que responde por correo a go@windoce.com en unos 2 días hábiles. El ticket se ve en
  Play Console → Ayuda → «Tus tickets de asistencia».
- Hasta que el especialista responda que sí, NO se crea ninguna app nueva (decisión de Richard).
- 15 nov 2026, 18:12 UTC: llegó el acuse automático de Google a go@windoce.com, caso 4-0869000041726. Todavía no es la respuesta del especialista.
