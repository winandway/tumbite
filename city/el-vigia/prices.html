Moto taxi fares in El Vigía

What a Divoox moto taxi costs from downtown El Vigía, by road, by day and by night. These are the same prices the app charges: what you see here is what the rider pays.

How you are charged

  • It is the most you pay for that trip: if the distance-based price comes out lower, the app charges the lower one.
  • You see the exact price before you request, and it does not change during the ride.
  • You pay in cash or by pago móvil bank transfer, straight to your driver.
  • At night, from 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM (El Vigía time), rides cost 30% more: the “At night” column already includes it.
  • A ride to a place that is not on the list is priced by distance, starting at $1.00.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a moto taxi in El Vigía?

With Divoox, by day, from $1.00 for a short ride up to $90.00 for the longest trip on the list, from downtown El Vigía. It is the most you pay: if the distance-based price comes out lower, the app charges the lower one.

How much more is it at night?

From 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM, El Vigía time, rides cost 30% more. The app shows it on the price card before you request.

How do I pay for the moto taxi?

You pay in cash or by pago móvil bank transfer, straight to your driver. You choose it before you request.

What if my destination is not on the list?

It is priced by distance, starting at $1.00. The app shows you the exact price before you request, and it does not change during the ride.
