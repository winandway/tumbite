Download the Divoox apps

One app for each person in the order. They’re free on Google Play for Android. Pick yours and tap “Download.”

  • Divoox

    To order food

    Browse restaurants near you, build your order, pay in the app, and follow your driver on the map to your door.

    AndroidAvailable on Google PlayiPhoneIn review by Apple

  • Divoox Driver

    To deliver

    Go online whenever you want, see what you’ll earn before you accept each order, and bring the food from the restaurant to the door.

    AndroidAvailable on Google PlayiPhoneIn review by Apple

  • Divoox Restaurants

    For your restaurant

    Get orders with an alert, accept them, mark them ready for the driver, and pause items when they run out.

    AndroidAvailable on Google PlayiPhoneIn review by Apple

On an Android phone, the button opens Google Play and you install it with one tap. From a computer, Google Play lets you send it to your phone.

On an iPhone? While Apple reviews the apps, open Divoox in Safari and add it to your Home Screen: it opens full screen, like an app. How to install Divoox from your browser
